# Source-verified role and development monitoring

The supported rollout is the current papal office and public health developments,
with equal EN/FR request support. Other roles and topics remain unsupported;
the evidence/resolution contract is reusable without pretending to support them.

Original role wording stays in interpretation/evidence. A separate `roleResolution`
in criteria records office, person URI, official EN/FR names, source and verification
time. The existing signed confirmation binds the complete definition. The issue
handler independently verifies the identity again. Ordinary named-subject Watches
and their stored definitions remain valid.

At each role check the holder is re-resolved from the unique ongoing pontificate
on the official Vatican EN/FR homepages. Names are never hardcoded or taken from
model memory. Conflicting pages, vacancy, ambiguity or unavailable verification
fail closed. Runtime Google News queries use the newly verified holder and papal
and health terms in EN/FR. The original approved role remains unchanged.

The existing RSS retrieval, public-URL safeguards, baseline, completion, identity
ledger and consent mechanisms are reused. Cadence, cron configuration, digest
worker and email gates do not change. Attribution comes from the feed's HTTPS
publisher metadata, restricted to Vatican, Reuters, AP and BBC origins.

The first baseline is silent and needs no classification. Subsequently at most
six newly observed, post-baseline-publication candidates are assessed in one
bounded call to the existing configured structured-output model: 1,400 maximum
output tokens, no retry/provider fallback, timeout bounded by the check deadline.
Article text is untrusted input. A verified development requires exact person,
development, attribution and event-date quotations from the supplied evidence.
Missing evidence, retrospective reporting, unrelated news and speculation do not
become alerts. Nothing infers a diagnosis. Only available title/excerpt evidence
is used; incomplete snippets can therefore cause conservative omissions.

An event date must be explicitly present or explicitly relative to publication
(today/yesterday/weekday, in EN/FR). Date-only evidence on the baseline's calendar
day is insufficient to establish occurrence after its exact timestamp; such
observations are retained without alerts. There is no rolling 24-hour cutoff.
Publication alone never establishes occurrence. A recent retrospective about an
older health event remains ineligible.

A second durable identity key uses verified holder, development type, reported
state and event day. Repeated coverage shares that key across article URLs and
uses the existing SQL identity ledger. This conservative grouping can merge
separate same-day reports of the same type/state; it is disclosed in confirmation
and does not promise exhaustive medical-event coverage. Exact source quotations
and runtime identity provenance remain in snapshots. No new event queue exists.

Staging delivery restrictions remain Royal Caribbean/Sinner only. This extension
does not enroll a papal Watch in email delivery, grant consent, activate a Watch,
or create a permanent live Watch without the user's confirmation.
